### Phase 3: Fix Round (Parallel, Isolated Worktrees)

All three agents continued in their own worktrees. Main remained clean during their work.

#### Amber (PR #8 → #8, commit `64d79cb`)

**Issue:** Linux CI failure — test hung on AsyncStream consumption.

**Root cause:** Single-consumer AsyncStream race. Both `WorkoutController.forwardingTask` and the test's `bridgeMetrics` subscriber iterated over `substrate.metricEvents` simultaneously. Linux strict task scheduler exposed it; macOS Darwin scheduler masked it.

**Fix:** Bridge from `controller.metrics` (controller's re-published stream) instead of direct substrate stream.

**Outcome:** ✅ PR #8 merged.
