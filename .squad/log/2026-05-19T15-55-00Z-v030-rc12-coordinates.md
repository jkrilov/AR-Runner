## Key Learning

### Off-Screen Clipping, Not Firmware Rejection

The rc11 blank at rotation=4, leftMargin=20 was caused by **coordinate out-of-bounds clipping**, not firmware rejection of the rotation byte. 

**The diagnostic recipe:**
- When a `txt` command goes blank with no 0xE2 error, first check spec §5.5.6: off-screen coordinates are **silently clipped** — no error thrown.
- Rotation interacts with anchor corner: `topLR` (rotation=4) anchors at TOP-RIGHT and extends LEFT and DOWN. Placing the anchor at small x values (e.g., x=20) puts the entire text block at negative x (off-screen).
- Before assuming firmware issue, verify the entire text bounding box stays inside framebuffer space (0..303 × 0..255 for Engo 2). Use the lens-flip formula `x_wearer = 303 − x_fb` to convert.

### All 8 Rotation Values Documented

The SDK enum `ActiveLookTypes.swift:41-50` documents all 8 rotation values. Prior skill note claiming "only 0 and 4 are official" was incorrect — all are valid; the selection depends on glyph orientation and anchor corner needed.
